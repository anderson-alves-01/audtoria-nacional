# IBS/CBS Readiness

Use only versioned official sources approved in the catalog. Store regulatory item, version, effective date, affected system, owner, test, evidence and status.

Keep dates, rates and layouts configurable. Produce readiness assessments and non-binding simulations. Never infer a legal obligation from model knowledge or outdated documents. Any rule used operationally requires municipal and legal homologation.
