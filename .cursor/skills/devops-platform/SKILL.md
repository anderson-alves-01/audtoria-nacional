# DevOps Platform

Provide Docker-based local development and Terraform modules for dev, staging, production and DR. Enforce least privilege, private networking, encryption, budgets, backups and observability. Separate API/web services from ingestion jobs.

CI must validate without cloud credentials. Terraform apply, production deploy, DNS, IAM grants and paid resources require explicit approval. Produce plans and rollback steps before any allowed mutation.
